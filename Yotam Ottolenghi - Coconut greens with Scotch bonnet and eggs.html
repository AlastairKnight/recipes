Coconut greens with Scotch bonnet and eggs

Yotam Ottolenghi

Prep 5 minsCook 25 minsServes 4

This recipe is inspired by the Trinidadian bhaji, which Milli Taylor, my test kitchen colleague, first discovered through her friend, the chef and food writer Shivi Ramoutar. Traditionally, these braised greens are served alongside rice and dal as a main meal, but we think they are equally delicious with the addition of eggs for breakfast. Play around with whatever greens are in season. Chard is great – just make sure you chop the stems and add them before the leaves, at the same time as the onions.

Method

  1. 1

    Heat the oil in a large saute pan on a medium-high heat. Add the onion and three-quarters of a teaspoon of salt and cook for about eight minutes, stirring often, until softened and golden. Add the cumin, Scotch bonnet, garlic and lots of pepper and cook for another minute. Add the spinach, in two or three batches, and cook for about three minutes until wilted. Add the coconut milk, bring to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes, until the sauce has thickened and reduced slightly.

  2. 2

    Use a spoon to make six wells in the mixture and crack one egg into each. Sprinkle the eggs with salt and pepper, cover, and cook for a further four to five minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny. Serve straight from the pan.

Getting ahead: if you want to get everything ready in advance, the dish can be prepared up to the point just before the eggs are cracked in.
