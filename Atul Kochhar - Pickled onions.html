Pickled onions

Atul Kochhar

Makes 250 g

Ingredients — tap to check off

Method

  1. 1

    Place the onions in a large heatproof, nonmetallic bowl, pour over enough boiling water to cover and leave to cool. Once they are cool, trim the roots and peel. Sprinkle salt over the onions and leave them overnight.

  2. 2

    The next day, rinse and dry the onions well, then set aside.

  3. 3

    Place the vinegar and honey in a saucepan over a medium heat, stirring to dissolve the honey. Do not boil.

  4. 4

    Pack the onions, beetroot, cinnamon, cloves, cardamoms and bay leaf in a hot, dry sterilized jar with a vinegar-proof lid. Pour over the hot vinegar mixture to fill the jar, making sure the onions are covered and there are no air pockets. Seal immediately and leave in a cool place for 2–3 weeks until the onions take on a rosy red colour. These will keep for up to 3 months in the fridge. Once opened, use within 2 weeks.
