Chicken simmered with juniper berries

Donna Hay

Serves 4

Ingredients — tap to check off

Method

  1. 1

    Place the chicken stock, juniper berries, verjuice and thyme in a deep frying pan over low heat and allow to simmer for 3 minutes.

  2. 2

    Add the chicken fillets to the pan and simmer for 6–7 minutes each side or until tender.

  3. 3

    Remove the chicken from the pan and keep warm.

  4. 4

    Increase the temperature to high and boil the pan liquid for 2 minutes or until it has thickened slightly.

  5. 5

    To serve, place the chicken on serving plates and top with the pan sauce. Serve with creamy mashed potato.
