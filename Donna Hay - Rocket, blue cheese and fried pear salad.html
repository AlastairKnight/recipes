Rocket, blue cheese and fried pear salad

Donna Hay

Serves 4

Ingredients — tap to check off

Method

  1. 1

    Brush bread with a little olive oil and sprinkle with parmesan. Place bread on a baking tray and cook in a preheated 180°C (350°F) oven for 15 minutes or until golden and crisp. Set aside to cool.

  2. 2

    Heat butter in a frypan over medium heat. Add brown sugar, pepper and coriander to pan and cook for 1 minute.

  3. 3

    Add pears to pan and cook for 2 minutes on each side or until golden.

  4. 4

    To serve, arrange crisp bread, rocket (arugula), cucumber, red onion and blue cheese on serving plates.

  5. 5

    Top with pears and pour pan juices from pears over salad. Sprinkle with cracked black pepper and serve immediately, while bread is crisp.
